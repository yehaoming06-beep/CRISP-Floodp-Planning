# Remaining Research Risks after CI-01 Integration

Date: 5 November 2026 (Asia/Shanghai). Scope: integration of the existing CI-01 evidence into the English method-validation manuscript. No additional experiment is prescribed or represented as completed in this revision.

**CI-01 remains PARTIALLY RESOLVED / NOT FULLY CLOSED.** The new reconstruction supplies a reproducible, bounded sampling frame and disclosed retrospective rules. It does not recover the historical selection procedure or validate an operational flood-response policy.

## 1. Evidence boundary that must remain visible

The final record contains 63 cyclone identities in 62 local-warning groups, cross-checked over 203 warning segments in 2017–2025. There are 60 numerically analyzable records, three pending identities and zero entity exclusions. All 23 historically selected identities are among the 60; 37 additional identities are analyzable. This arithmetic is established by the candidate and inclusion ledgers, not by an assumed selection threshold. The HKO database concerns local tropical-cyclone warnings; it is not a census of Tai O inundation, all tropical-cyclone rainfall, remote cyclone effects or non-cyclonic floods. Absence from this frame does not establish absence of impact. See the [HKO warning database](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/wxinfo/climat/warndb/warndb1.shtml) and the retained `audit/ci01_independent_20261104/evidence/Candidate_Completeness_Check.json`.

## 3. What has been resolved within scope

The bounded local-warning register, explicit new rules, all-candidate disposition ledger, numerical reconstruction of the old 23, source-conflict disclosure, matched-station comparison, conditional-frequency sensitivity and existing replication logs materially strengthen transparency. The frozen 2017–2025 rainfall responses agree 27/27 by bytes, and the previous final-verification record reports 102 deterministic outputs, two matching 555-row optimization summaries and unchanged hashes for 977 frozen files. These are computational and provenance checks; they do not close the open historical, measurement, representativeness or calibration issues above. The integration verification separately states which checks were repeated in this revision.
