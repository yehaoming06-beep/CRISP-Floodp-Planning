## 3 Conditions that would create a genuine scientific submission blocker

The following would require correction or a narrower claim before submission if found in the final candidate: an infeasible reported policy; a primary objective inconsistent with its saved inputs; EEV that reoptimizes a first-stage decision; a false WS/RP/EEV ordering beyond numerical tolerance; unsupported numerical transcription; unresolved source identity supporting an essential claim; contradiction between the abstract and actual analytical population; fabricated coefficients/declarations/experiments; or an empirical/policy conclusion for which the only evidence is illustrative model scores. None should be waived because a solver log says PASS.

The reviewed supplementary records do not demonstrate those failures. Their negative controls are coherent: finite stock restricts the attainable score; replicated and shared stock can have equal optima despite different feasible sets; procurement integrality alone is structurally neutral with integer deployments; and k = 6 deployment coverage is deliberately infeasible. These results narrow attribution rather than invalidate the conditional formulation.
