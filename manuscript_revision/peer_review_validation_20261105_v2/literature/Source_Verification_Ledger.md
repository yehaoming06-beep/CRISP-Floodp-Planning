## Verification interpretation

This is a methodological prior-art comparison, not a systematic clinical evidence review. Medical evidence levels I–VII are not assigned mechanically to optimisation papers. Source confidence instead rests on primary identity, exact version, observable model passages, and explicit access limits. No claim is made to have checked every venue in Scopus/DOAJ, screened a retraction database, audited authors' conflicts of interest, or independently verified reported numerical performance. Missing access is **UNVERIFIABLE_ACCESS for unobserved detail**, not fabrication and not evidence of absence.

The prior local `Citation_Audit.md` was inspected as context: its Rawls/Mete rows were already preview-level. The present ledger performs fresh primary-source lookup and preserves that limit. It does not promote the entire prior bibliography to a full-text audit and does not change CI-01, CI-05 or CI-06.
